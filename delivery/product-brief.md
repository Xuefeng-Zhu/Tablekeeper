# Tablekeeper product brief

Deliver a dependable restaurant reservation product: browse before authentication, book without duplicate occupancy, recover uncertain submissions, and retain confirmation identities across upgrades. Managers gain dated policies and deterministic seating repairs; diners gain recurring agreements.

Stage 1 delivers only the complete HTTP API, authentication, atomic bookings and moves, precise timezone handling, idempotency and portable state. Stage 2 copies forward only an independently accepted Stage 1 and adds approved pairs and a polished responsive booking/lookup UI. Stage 3 adds policy decisions, immutable accepted terms, history and recurrence. Stage 4 adds optimal seating plans with atomic application and recurring amendments. Each stage remains an independent container and source tree.

Required behavior is the exact pinned official specification; delivery/requirements.json maps every second-level section to its immutable source and independent evidence target. Sources stay outside the deliverable. No optional dashboards, external integrations, paid provisioning or decorative asset work is scheduled.

Architecture decisions precede implementation. Designer journeys and state specifications precede browser implementation. QA owns independent behavior tests; Reviewer owns exact-candidate release acceptance in a clean temporary checkout. A failed gate blocks advancement. No stage directory is claimed complete before acceptance.

The root README.md and FACTORY.md are reserved for post-run human narratives; room.json must be a genuine complete BAND download. Missing final metadata is not a passing packaging result.
